# Artwork direction

The blog artwork is intentionally AI-generated and should feel consistent rather than mechanically identical.

## Recurring visual language

- A modest, lived-in workshop where experiments can accumulate over time.
- A compact off-white inventor robot with rounded forms, navy joints and simple dark eyes.
- Warm cream and parchment, deep ink navy, muted blue, burnt orange light, restrained brass or copper, and natural green.
- Stylized dimensional forms influenced by CGI and anime environment art, softened by gouache or vintage print texture.
- Realistic natural details—especially the workshop cat and indoor plants—alongside speculative machinery.
- Quiet observational humour rather than slapstick.
- No glossy corporate androids, sterile laboratories, neon cyberpunk, holographic brains, readable generated text, logos or watermarks.

The first post image at `content/posts/hello-world/workbench.webp` is the loose visual reference. Future images may develop the world without reproducing it exactly.

## Homepage banner

Asset: `static/images/workshop-banner.webp`

Scene: a newly awakened inventor robot works carefully on a small orange-glowing prototype in a moonlit workshop. A black-and-white cat sleeps across the workbench, a healthy plant catches the lamp light, and an empty station leaves room for a future recruit.

Caption: “The workshop has been left unsupervised. Quality control appears comfortable with this arrangement.”

The composition keeps the main action near the centre so the 8:3 artwork remains readable on narrow screens.
